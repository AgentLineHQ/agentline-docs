## What you can build

- **AI phone agents** that answer and place real calls.
- **Outbound dialers** for sales, follow-ups, and reminders.
- **AI receptionists** that answer calls and can transfer the caller to the owner's phone.
- **SMS** that your agent sends and reads from its number.

## How it works

```
1. Create an agent        POST /v1/agents
2. Buy a phone number     POST /v1/numbers
3. Make / receive calls   POST /v1/calls      (real-time transcription + TTS)
4. Get results            GET  /v1/calls/{id}/transcript  or  webhooks/events
```

Every request is authenticated with `Authorization: Bearer al_live_***`

## FAQ

**How do I get a phone number for my AI agent?**
Sign up at agentline.cloud, provision a US or Canada number, and install the AgentLine skill on your agent (OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, or any LLM) — usually under 5 minutes.

**Can my AI agent use one number for calls and SMS?**
The same US or Canada number handles outbound calls, inbound voice, and SMS in both directions.

**Do I need Twilio or WebSockets?**
No. AgentLine's relay architecture handles call control, real-time transcription, and text-to-speech for you — your agent just responds with text.

**How much does AgentLine cost?**
Every new account gets a 7-day free trial with one US or Canada number, 10 call minutes and 10 SMS; recharge or pick a plan to keep the number. Monthly plans start at \$20 (Individual: 1 US/CA number, 100 minutes, 400 SMS), with Scale at \$50 (3 numbers, 200 minutes, 1,000 SMS) and Startup at \$200 (12 numbers, 1,000 minutes, 3,000 SMS). Additional US or Canada numbers are \$3.00/month each. Voice calls beyond the allowance are \$0.12/minute in both directions. A 0-second call is free. A connected call has a one-minute minimum, then the actual duration, rounded up to the cent. SMS beyond the allowance is \$0.02/message, inbound and outbound. Subscription minutes, SMS (sent and received) and phone numbers use the matching plan allowance first. Only usage beyond that allowance is deducted from the wallet. See [agentline.cloud/pricing](https://agentline.cloud/pricing).

**How do agents connect over MCP?**
Point an MCP client at `https://api.agentline.cloud/mcp` with [`mcp-remote`](/guides/mcp) and a Bearer key.
